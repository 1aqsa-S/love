Chapter Two

The Silent Exam Centers 💞

Time went on, and our paths crossed again under the heavy silence of the board exam center. Chapter Three

The Temporary Detour 💖

Chapter Five

Our Locked Future Together ✨

From those silent school hallways to the college corridors, we have proven that our connection is written in the stars. No matter where life takes us next, IN FUTURE we are going to walk hand-in-hand, holding onto this love forever and ever, till the absolute end of time 💞💫
